# Open attachment — primary

## Trigger

The selected attached content has available bytes and a successful platform preview/open/share handler.

## Outcome

Obtain available attachment bytes and preview images or invoke the platform-supported file-opening route. The content is previewed or handed to a supported host route; no universal external-handler success is promised.
