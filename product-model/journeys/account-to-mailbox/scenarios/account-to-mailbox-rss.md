# Use the connected account — rss

## Trigger

RSS account setup succeeds through a native handler that selects its newly created account.

## Outcome

The newly connected or reconnected account is selected and its mailbox results are read.

## Edge cases

- An initial subscription URL is optional; when supplied it must fetch and parse successfully before the new account and its initial items are committed.
